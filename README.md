## how it works

- 24 turns, 2 actions per turn (3 on easy)
- pick a borough and enact policy cards from your hand
- keep budget, affordability, ridership, and approval up
- policies need approval from the mta, city council, or albany
- demands, events, and borough issues come up each turn
- your score is citywide approval, so dont let one borough rot

## features

- three difficulties: easy, normal, and hard
- dozens of policy cards and random events
- autosave and continue, so you can quit mid-term
- best approval saved for each difficulty
- achievements for finishing terms in different ways
- sound effects with a mute button on the title screen

## controls

- click a borough on the map to target it
- 1 to 5 enacts the card in that slot
- space or enter ends the turn
- n targets the neediest borough
- escape opens the pause menu
